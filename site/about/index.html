About Bright Penny

We make introductions. That's the whole job.

Finding business finance usually starts with a search engine and ends in a fog of comparison sites and call centres. We keep it to one conversation and one introduction, to a firm we know.

The introducer model

Three parties, clearly separated.

An introducer connects a business that needs finance with a firm that can arrange it. That's the entire role. We don't lend, we don't broker deals, and we don't advise on what to take.

When you enquire, we ask enough to understand what you're after. Then, with your consent, we pass your details to our finance partner. From that point you deal with PMD Business Finance directly: the options, the terms and the arranging all happen with them, from first call to completion.

  1. Your business Tells us what it needs
  2. Bright Penny Makes the introduction With your consent
  3. PMD Business Finance Discusses your options and arranges the facility, directly with you

Where the line sits.

What we do

  • Listen to what your business is trying to fund
  • Make the introduction to PMD Business Finance, with your consent
  • Tell you exactly who has your details, and why

What we don't

  • Lend money or arrange finance ourselves
  • Give financial advice or recommend products
  • Pass your details to marketing lists

The partnership

The finance side is PMD Business Finance.

Every introduction goes to PMD Business Finance, an independent commercial finance firm based in Greater Manchester that works with businesses across the UK.

They cover the nine finance types described on this site, from asset finance through to structured deals, working with lenders that range from high street banks to specialist funders. [This section to be reviewed and approved by PMD Business Finance before launch.]
